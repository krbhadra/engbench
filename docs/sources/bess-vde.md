# BESS design chain — source survey, pass 2: VDE / DIN VDE / IEC rule set

Scope: 1500 V DC LFP BESS with MV grid connection in Germany. Companion to `bess.md` (US carriers); this
pass replaces the NEC/IEEE/UL chain with the German/European one. VDE, DIN VDE and IEC documents are
copyrighted: they are cited by number only and the METHOD is implemented from a public carrier that
states it. Numeric values (ampacities, correction factors, fuse data, derating) are taken from vendor
catalogues, network-operator guidelines, association guides or test reports that reproduce them.
"Verified" = the document was fetched (PDF text extracted locally with pdftotext) and the values below
were read from its text on 2026-09-18. Openness codes: VEN = vendor document (vendor copyright; facts and
short attributed phrases only); VEN-DIN = vendor document that reproduces excerpts of a DIN VDE table
"mit Genehmigung des DIN/VDE" (cite the vendor document, never the standard table itself); NB = network
operator (Netzbetreiber) guideline, public; ASSOC = association guide with explicit non-commercial
reproduction permission; TR = test report with explicit reproduction permission; PRESS = trade press
article; PREVIEW = standards-body public preview (scope/foreword only).

## 3. Knowledge traps specific to the VDE / IEC rule set

1. Fuse-vs-cable coordination is not I_n ≤ I_z: DIN VDE 0100-430 requires I_2 ≤ 1.45 I_z, and a gG fuse's I_2 is 1.6 I_n, so I_n ≤ 0.906 I_z (equivalently I_z ≥ 1.10 I_n). MCBs (I_2 = 1.45 I_n) satisfy it automatically (#4, #5). Applying the MCB shortcut to a fuse is the classic error.
2. Ampacity base is 30 °C in air and 20 °C in ground (DIN VDE 0298-4 / 0276-603), and ABB's Table 3 re-bases German practice to 25 °C. Mixing a 25 °C table with 30 °C correction factors, or using the 30 °C factors on a ground table, gives wrong I_z (#1, #3).
3. A DC pole pair is "2 belastete Adern": the 2-conductor column (e.g. 232 A vs 196 A for 70 mm², method E) applies, not the three-phase column; single-core DC cables with a distant return conductor have their own (higher) rating per DIN VDE 0276-603 (#1, #3).
4. Terminal temperature: in building installations 90 °C XLPE cable may only be loaded so the conductor stays ≤ 70 °C (0298-4 C.3.2); using the 90 °C column at a 70 °C terminal is over-rating (#1).
5. Grouping counts circuits, not cables: n parallel single-core DC cables per pole are n/2 two-conductor circuits; the factor drops to 0.70 at 3 bundled circuits and 0.48 at 10 (#1, #2, #3).
6. DC fuse voltage ratings are only valid at their rated time constant: gBat NH ratings are given at L/R ≤ 1–3 ms (Mersen, SIBA, DF), ETI battery fuses at 10 ms; a circuit with larger L/R needs a fuse rated at that L/R. A 690 V AC gG fuse has no usable 1500 V DC rating and only ~2 I_n minimum breaking capacity on DC (#7, #8, #9, #11).
7. gBat ≠ aBat ≠ gPV: gBat is full-range (minimum breaking current 2 I_n), aBat is back-up only (needs BMS/contactor for overloads), gPV (IEC 60269-6) is for PV strings and is not a battery category (#7, #8, #10).
8. IEC 60269-7 sets a 30 kA minimum breaking capacity, but a 1500 V bus fed by many parallel racks plus the PCS can exceed 100 kA; check against the fuse's stated 100–260 kA and the PCS's 250 kA @ 3 ms figure (#8, #11, #27).
9. The gG "1.6 : 1 rating ratio" selectivity rule is an AC gG rule (Mersen EduPack); gBat/aBat selectivity is checked by I²t (SIBA), and rack-vs-module coordination can run "in reverse" for bus faults (#4, #8, bess.md #5).
10. cos φ 0.95/0.95 is required at the Netzanschlusspunkt in every operating point (block format, Q/P_b,inst = 0.33), so S_PCS must cover P/0.95 plus the reactive losses of transformer and cables downstream of the PCS; sizing the PCS at P/0.95 exactly fails at full load (#17, #18, #19). At LV the number is 0.90 above 4.6 kVA (#22). Charging is cos φ = 1 by default (#17, #21).
11. Storage is treated as an Erzeugungsanlage for reactive power at MV (both TABs and M.O.E.), including on charging when the Q(U) mode is commanded (#17, #19).
12. Dry-type transformer basis is 40 °C max / 30 °C monthly / 20 °C annual (IEC 60076-11 via GEAFOL, Trihal); the derating key is the annual mean (93 % at 30 °C annual mean), and altitude > 1000 m is a "special design", not a catalogue factor — a model that reuses the IEEE/Hammond altitude table without saying so is mixing rule sets (#23, #24).
13. IEC 61660-1 is a lead-acid model (0.9 R_b, T_B = 30 ms, 0.95 E); applying it to LFP requires manufacturer R_i/L_i and a stated assumption; the E/R_i rule of thumb over-predicts vs. measurements only in some tests and under-predicts in others (#25, #26).
14. VDE-AR-E 2510-50 is scoped to "private and small commercial" systems; for a 1500 V utility BESS it is a reference document, and the governing safety set is EN IEC 62619 (7.3.3 propagation), IEC 62933-5-2 (IEC-only; EN version not expected), 62485-5, 63056, 62477-1 plus the BVES guide. Its numeric limits are manufacturer-defined (cell T_max, derating curves), so a grader can only check the presence of functions, not values (#12, #13, #14).
15. PCS kVA derates from 40 °C (Multi PCSK) or 50 °C (PCSK, SMA at 50 °C), the DC window minimum rises with AC over-voltage, and the AC output may have to be capped to satisfy inverter-level P–Q capability in some grid codes (#27, bess.md #3).
16. Voltage drop: DIN VDE 0100-520 Annex G is informative; a BESS behind its own MV transformer is a "private supply" case (6 %/8 %), not the 3 %/5 % public-supply case; a task must state which limit it grades against (#6).

## 4. Unverified / caveats

- 90 °C (XLPE) base ampacities for 70–300 mm² in a public carrier: not found this pass. ABB #1 covers PVC 70 °C to 120 mm²; Helukabel #3 building tables stop at 10 mm² and the NYY/N2XY tables at 50 mm² in the extracted edition. A task needing 150–300 mm² XLPE must supply the table values itself or restrict cross-sections.
- Helukabel DC column: the "Bemessungsstrom in Gleichstromanlagen mit weit entferntem Rückleiter" values were assigned by column position in the extracted layout; confirm against the PDF rendering before use in a grader.
- Lapp Table 12-12 (J_thr): row/column alignment in the text extraction is unreliable; treat the table as the carrier for the adiabatic method but read values from the rendered PDF.
- Continuous-load factor for DC fuses (I_b ≤ 0.8 I_n or similar): no IEC/European carrier states it; Mersen only tabulates losses at 0.7 I_n; the factors remain the pass-1 US carriers (Littelfuse F_L 0.75, Mersen A'2 0.7). Eaton IEC guide CA135002EN could not be read (timeout).
- IEC 60269-7 Tables 101/104/105 (conventional currents, test time constants) are not public; only the scope and the existence of the tables are verified.
- SIBA flyer selectivity inequality ("I²t_A rack fuse << I²t_s module fuse"): the subscript convention (A = Ausschalt/total clearing, s = Schmelz/pre-arcing) and direction are not explained in the flyer; treat as vendor shorthand.
- IEC 60076-11 altitude derating factor: no public carrier verified (GEAFOL says special design; Trihal 2018 catalogue, EEP article and IJAREEIE paper returned 403). Trihal 1994 ambient table is truncated after 35 °C in the extraction.
- VDE-AR-N 4110 part-load threshold: M.O.E. states "> 20 % P_N" for the 33 % block, the FNN draft summary states 0.05 < P_mom/P_b,inst; the final text was not read. Bayernwerk Anwendungshinweise (2026) and VDE FNN Stellungnahmen E VDE-AR-N 4110:2024-11 were not readable (403 / not fetched).
- No public VDE-AR-N 4110 statement about a DC time constant was found; the DC L/R convention comes only from PCS and fuse datasheets (≤ 1 ms SMA, 3 ms PCSK/Mersen/SIBA, 10 ms ETI).
- VDE-AR-E 2510-50 clause titles come from the SGS test-report form (2023) and the DKE abstract; the rule text was not read. BVES file: the URL is labelled "EN 2023" but serves the German 3rd edition (2025).

- Multi PCSK / PCSK datasheets list IEC 62109 / 62477 but no VDE-AR-N 4110/4120 or EN 50549 certificate; Sungrow PowerTitan 2.0 lists G99 and "IEC 62933" without part numbers. Intilion (#30) and Tesvolt PCS (#29) do list VDE-AR-N 4110.
